Why FreshFind exists

Making local food easier to discover.

FreshFind is a simple digital guide to farmers markets, fresh produce and the people who make local food possible.

Our story

Local food should be easy to find.

Markets are full of good things, but information about where they are, when they open and what they carry can be scattered. FreshFind brings those details together in one friendly place.

We designed the platform around a simple idea: help people discover local food while giving neighbourhood markets a clearer digital front door.
